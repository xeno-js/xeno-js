# Xeno.JS Roadmap

Our goal is to build an extensible ecosystem of adapters around the Xeno.JS application core.

## Planned official adapters

* [ ] **Hono adapter** — HTTP integration for applications using Hono.
* [ ] **SQLite adapter** — SQLite database integration.

## Community contributions

Community members are welcome to propose and develop additional adapters and integrations.

Before starting a new adapter, open an issue or discussion to align on its scope and integration contract.

## Official package policy

Official `@xeno-js/*` packages must follow the project's adapter conventions, pass the required tests, and be reviewed by the maintainers before being accepted and released.

Community-maintained packages are also welcome. They may be published independently, provided they clearly identify their maintainers and compatibility with Xeno.JS.
